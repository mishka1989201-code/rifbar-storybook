# ClientOrders (prototype)

The client page, *Orders* tab, at seven widths, with the filter screens of the small widths. Implements the Figma frame
[`Filter Chevrons / Filter Menu Responsive`](https://www.figma.com/design/4Q7E8IQ07a9xFiNVBfmo4M/%F0%9F%93%B1-ERP-System-v-1.1--Mockups----Rifbar-2023%F0%9F%93%B1?node-id=818-310893)
(`Manager User - Clients - All Clients Tab - Details (Orders Tab) (UI)`).

A **prototype** is a whole screen assembled only from library components, with fake in-memory data. It is not exported from `rifbar-ds`;
it shows how the components fit together at each breakpoint. Components that were new for this screen live in the library:
[TableRowOrder](?path=/docs/molecules-tableroworder--docs), [TableOrders](?path=/docs/organisms-tableorders--docs),
[TableToolbar](?path=/docs/molecules-tabletoolbar--docs); extended: [PageHeader](?path=/docs/molecules-pageheader--docs),
[BreadCrumbs](?path=/docs/molecules-breadcrumbs--docs), [CheckListModal](?path=/docs/molecules-checklistmodal--docs),
[TableHeader](?path=/docs/molecules-tableheader--docs), [PaginationBar](?path=/docs/molecules-paginationbar--docs).

## Behaviour (what is interactive)

Search, status filter (menu, chips, the filter screen), date range (calendar under the field), sorting (header and sort menu), page size,
pages, the view switch, the burger menu and the open / close of the filter screen all work on the fake data (30 orders; the first 11 are the Figma rows).
The “More” buttons, the bell and the tabs do nothing but switch the active tab.

## Figma notes

What Figma draws: the screen at 1920 / 1440 / 1280 / 1024 / 768 / 480 / 360px; the Navbar only at 1920 and 1440; the filter menu at 768 / 480 / 360px (Status + Date range) and the *All Filters* version of it; two small menus (`Sort by`, `Status Filter`).

Not drawn, defined by analogy (AI-defined):

- Empty result, loading and error of the list (`TableOrders` states), hover / focus / disabled of every control (from the atoms).
- The side menu opening over the content below 1440px (only its closed state is drawn); the Navbar `size` follows the frame.
- The “View” switch on desktop: the cards view reuses the 768px card grid.
- The open Date range calendar on desktop (the `DatePicker` range, two months).
- Dark theme: not designed — the light values are used.

Assumed / normalised — **need designer confirmation**:

- The “View” switch is drawn at 768px and below, but the content there is always cards; here both views show cards below 1024px.
- The page padding changes per frame (32 / 27 / 24 / 16 / 16 / 16 / 10px). 27px is mapped to `--spacing-28`; the right margin of the 1920px frame is 32px, the others 16px.
- Tabs at 360px: Figma squeezes three tabs into 340px; the `TabsHeader` keeps its 24px padding and scrolls (the last tab is cut).
- The date range in the filter screen starts empty (Figma shows 16–25 March selected).
- The card text switches between price / type and client / phone from row to row only to show both Figma variants (`TableRowOrder`).
- The text under the title of the filter screen (“You have selected one product category…”) is an unused leftover in Figma and is not drawn.
- Figma pictures: none are used on this screen (no asset download was needed).

## Accessibility

- One `main` landmark with one `h1` (`PageHeader`); the side menu is a `nav`; the toolbar is a `toolbar`; tables and cards are `role="table"` with rows; the filter menus are native buttons, checkboxes and radios; the pick lists are `listbox`es.
- The menus (`Popover`) close on outside click and `Esc`; focus is not moved into them and not trapped — add that in a real screen. The side menu overlay has no focus trap either.
- Contrast: see the components used (header `#4549A1` on `#F6F8FC` 7.26:1; row text 14.68:1; chips 4.59:1 – 15.05:1).
- ⚠️ Kept as designed: controls of 32–40px are below the 44px touch target; the cards are separated from the page by a shadow only.
