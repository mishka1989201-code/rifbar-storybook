# TimeTracker / TimeScale

A day scale: a pill split into hour parts, worked periods in green, hour labels below. Implements the Figma component
[`TimeTracker/Time Scale and Numbers`](https://www.figma.com/design/4Q7E8IQ07a9xFiNVBfmo4M/ERP-System-v-1.1--Mockups----Rifbar-2023?node-id=662-271469) (1492px wide in the mockup, here 100%).
The time tracker is built from four molecules: [Title](?path=/docs/molecules-timetracker-title--docs), [Date](?path=/docs/molecules-timetracker-date--docs), [Bar](?path=/docs/molecules-timetracker-bar--docs) (Title + Date) and [TimeScale](?path=/docs/molecules-timetracker-timescale--docs).

## Figma → code mapping

| Figma | Code |
|---|---|
| `Time Scale` (25px, radius 30, 12 × `Part` Stroke Light V2 with a 2px BG Color line) | `.ds-time-scale__track`, `hours` parts |
| Two green raster rectangles (195px and 19px) | `segments`: `[{ start: 8, end: 9.6 }, { start: 10, end: 10.15 }]` (hours of the day) |
| `Time Numbers` (Medium h7, Headlines, 2 lines: “08:00” / “am”) | labels, from `startHour` to `startHour + hours` |

## When to use

- ✅ Showing when during a working day time was tracked.

## When NOT to use

- ❌ Progress of a task or upload — use [Slider](?path=/docs/atoms-slider--docs) or a progress bar.

The `--color-*` rows above are light values; the dark values come from the themed tokens listed in the last rows.

Needs confirmation:
- The green is a raster image in Figma; the gradient is sampled from the render (left light green → right dark green on each period).
- Figma labels the 5th tick “12:00 am” and the next “01:00 pm”. Here the labels are computed, so noon is “12:00 pm”.
- Period positions are my reading of the mockup (about 08:00 – 09:36 and 10:00 – 10:09).

AI-defined: any `startHour` / `hours`, clipping of periods to the scale, `role="img"` with a text summary.

## Accessibility

- The scale is one image with a text summary (`aria-label`: range and worked periods); the visible labels are hidden from screen readers.
- Dark theme contrast: labels 7.13:1.
