# CallMenu

A small bubble with an arrow on top and two ways to reach a client: **WhatsApp** and **A phone call**. Implements the Figma component
[`tooltip-call`](https://www.figma.com/design/4Q7E8IQ07a9xFiNVBfmo4M/%F0%9F%93%B1-ERP-System-v-1.1--Mockups----Rifbar-2023%F0%9F%93%B1?node-id=5318-243539). It opens under the phone button of a clients table row
([TableRowClient](?path=/docs/molecules-tablerowclient--docs), prop `callMenu`).

## Figma notes

What Figma draws: one static bubble, the call option filled darker than the WhatsApp option (read as the hovered / pressed one).

Not drawn, defined by analogy (AI-defined):

- Resting fill of both options is Stroke Light V1; hover and pressed use Stroke Light V2 (the Figma "Call" fill).
- Keyboard focus: a 2px Hover Blue Light ring.
- Dark theme: no dark frame; values by analogy with TooltipBordered (`--call-menu-*` tokens, listed below).
- The WhatsApp glyph could not be downloaded (asset proxy 403); the icon `whatsapp` is drawn by hand — **needs designer confirmation**.
- Open / close and position are the caller's job (the table row shows it on hover and focus of the phone button).

## Accessibility

- `role="menu"` with two `role="menuitem"` buttons; native buttons, so Tab and Enter / Space work.
- Label contrast is Primary Blue Dark on Stroke Light V1 / V2 (13.48:1 and 11.49:1 in the light theme; 8.57:1 and 9.05:1 in the dark one, calculated by script); the white glyph on the Green Dark circle is 4.65:1.
- ⚠️ The bubble stroke Stroke Button on white is 1.64:1 (kept as designed).
- ⚠️ The options are 38px high, below the 44px touch target (kept as designed).
