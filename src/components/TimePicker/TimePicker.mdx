# TimePicker

Compact time picker: the chosen time (“01:20 PM”) and two sliders — hours on top, minutes below.
Implements the Figma frame [`Time picker`](https://www.figma.com/design/4Q7E8IQ07a9xFiNVBfmo4M/ERP-System-v-1.1--Mockups----Rifbar-2023?node-id=3923-227845). It is composed of two [Slider](?path=/docs/atoms-slider--docs) atoms.

## When to use

- ✅ Quick, approximate time selection in a small panel (time tracker, filters, forms with a narrow column).

## When NOT to use

- ❌ Exact time entry or keyboard typing of a time — use an input field.

## Figma notes

- Figma shows one frame and does not name what the two sliders control. They are read from the picture as hours (top) and minutes (bottom): the handle positions fit 13/23 and 20/59 for “01:20 PM”. **To be confirmed by a designer.**
- Figma draws the sliders as 18px rows with the handle overflowing the track; the `Slider` atom has a 48px touch area, so inside this molecule its height is cut to 18px (2px track + 8px padding ×2). Touch area is therefore smaller than in the atom.
- Hours are 0–23, shown in the 12-hour format with AM / PM (12:xx PM at noon, 12:xx AM at midnight). No 24-hour mode is designed.
- Figma colour `M3/sys/light/surface` (#FFFBFE) exists only as the unused frame fill — the component is transparent.
- Not designed, AI-defined: digits are tabular (`font-variant-numeric`) so the value does not jitter; `minuteStep`; `disabled` (atom's 20% opacity); focus ring comes from the `Slider` atom. Dark theme not designed — light values are used.

## Accessibility

- Each slider is a native `<input type="range">` with an `aria-label` (“Hours”, “Minutes”); keyboard arrows / Home / End work. The displayed time is `aria-live="polite"`.
- Slider rows are 18px high — below the 44px touch target, kept as designed.
- Secondary Grey `#A7A4B7` (AM / PM) on white is below 3:1 contrast; Not Active `#D2D3D9` track is 1.5:1 — kept as designed.
- Disabled at 20% opacity is below 3:1.
- Dark theme contrast: time 12.56:1; am/pm `#646464` 3.55:1 (⚠️ below 4.5:1, as designed).
