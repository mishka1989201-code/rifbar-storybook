# RegionSwitch

Switches the **region the business is run in** — United States or Europe. The clients list and the other settings of the app depend on it.
Lives in the page header, before the bell. Implements the `toggle` of the Figma `Header Menu`
([`Clients`](https://www.figma.com/design/4Q7E8IQ07a9xFiNVBfmo4M/%F0%9F%93%B1-ERP-System-v-1.1--Mockups----Rifbar-2023%F0%9F%93%B1?node-id=5640-252833)).

## Figma → code mapping

| Figma | Code |
|---|---|
| `UM - United States Minor Outlying Islands`, 32×24, radius 2 | left flag, `Region` `'us'` |
| `atom`: 48×24 pill, Secondary Grey stroke, knob 18px | `role="switch"` button |
| `Flag_of_Europe 1`, 32×24, radius 2 | right flag, `Region` `'eu'` |
| Gap 10 between the three; 24 to the bell | `gap` of the component; the page header gap |

The app owns the state: `value` + `onChange(region)` (or `defaultValue`). `Region = 'us' | 'eu'`.

## When to use

- ✅ In the page header of every screen whose data depends on the region (clients, orders …).

## When NOT to use

- ❌ Not a language switch and not a theme switch (see [Switcher](?path=/docs/atoms-switcher--docs)).

## Figma notes

- Figma draws only the US state (knob left). **The EU state is AI-defined**: the knob moves to the right.
- The flag vectors could not be downloaded (asset proxy 403): the flags are drawn in SVG from the measured colours; the stars of the US flag are simplified (a grid of dots), the EU star yellow is the official `#FFCC00`.
- Hover and focus are not drawn: focus ring like the other header controls (Headlines outline).
- Dark theme: no dark frame; values by analogy with `Toggle` (AI-defined). The flags keep their colours.
- A click on a flag selects its region too (not in Figma).

## Dark theme

No Figma dark frame shows the switch. AI-defined: pill `White (Dark)`, stroke `Secondary Grey (Dark)`, knob `Hover Blue Light`.

## Code example

```tsx
const [region, setRegion] = useState<Region>('us');

<HeaderMenu>
  <RegionSwitch value={region} onChange={setRegion} />
  <HeaderMenuItem icon="bell" label="Notifications" dot="warning" />
</HeaderMenu>
```

## Accessibility

- Three buttons: the flags (`aria-pressed`, named "United States" / "Europe") and the pill (`role="switch"`, `aria-checked` = Europe, named "Region: …"). Keyboard: Tab, Space / Enter.
- ⚠️ The pill is 48×24 and the flags 32×24, below the 44px touch target (kept as designed).
- ⚠️ The pill stroke `#A7A4B7` on white is below 3:1 (kept as designed). The state is also shown by the knob position, not by colour only.
