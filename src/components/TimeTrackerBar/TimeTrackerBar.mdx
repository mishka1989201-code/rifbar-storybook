# TimeTracker / Bar

The top bar of the time tracker: [Title](?path=/docs/molecules-timetracker-title--docs) on the left,
[Date](?path=/docs/molecules-timetracker-date--docs) on the right. Implements the Figma component
[`TimeTracker/Play Actions Menu`](https://www.figma.com/design/4Q7E8IQ07a9xFiNVBfmo4M/ERP-System-v-1.1--Mockups----Rifbar-2023?node-id=662-271087) (1492px wide in the mockup, here 100%).
The time tracker is built from four molecules: [Title](?path=/docs/molecules-timetracker-title--docs), [Date](?path=/docs/molecules-timetracker-date--docs), [Bar](?path=/docs/molecules-timetracker-bar--docs) (Title + Date) and [TimeScale](?path=/docs/molecules-timetracker-timescale--docs).

## Dark theme

Figma "Dark Molecules Components" → `Play Actions Menu Dark`: bar `#000000` with a `#242424` bottom line (AI-defined; Figma draws none) — `--time-tracker-bg` / `--time-tracker-bar-border`. Story: `DefaultDark`.

## Design tokens used

| Token | Property | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-white` | background | `#FFFFFF` | Figma: White |
| `--spacing-16` | padding, gap | `16px` | Figma: p 16 |
| `--color-stroke-light-v1` / `--border-width-sm` | bottom line | `#F1F2F9` / `1px` | Figma: Stroke Light V1 |
| `--time-tracker-bg` / `--time-tracker-bar-border` | bar, bottom line | white, `#F1F2F9` / dark `#000000`, `#242424` | **New** · Figma: Play Actions Menu Dark (line: AI-defined) |

The `--color-*` rows above are light values; the dark values come from the themed tokens listed in the last rows.

Everything inside comes from the two parts. No new tokens. AI-defined: wrapping on a narrow bar (the date block goes under the title).
