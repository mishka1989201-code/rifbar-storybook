# DatePicker

A calendar panel to pick a day, a day with a time, or a range of days, with a `Cancel` / `Apply` bar (or a single `Today` button). Implements the Figma frame
[`date-range-apply`](https://www.figma.com/design/4Q7E8IQ07a9xFiNVBfmo4M/%F0%9F%93%B1-ERP-System-v-1.1--Mockups----Rifbar-2023%F0%9F%93%B1?node-id=3922-225782) (four variants) and is an **Organism**, composed from
[Button](?path=/docs/atoms-button--docs), [Icon](?path=/docs/atoms-icon--docs) and [TimePicker](?path=/docs/molecules-timepicker--docs).
Days are real buttons in an ARIA grid; the calendar logic (month grid, range, keyboard) is part of the component.

## When to use

- ✅ Picking a date or a date range in a popover or a filter; `withTime` for a date and time of an event.

## When NOT to use

- ❌ A date typed by hand — use [InputField](?path=/docs/atoms-inputfield--docs).
- ❌ Only a time — use [TimePicker](?path=/docs/molecules-timepicker--docs).

## Behaviour

- `value` / `defaultValue` is the **draft**: `onChange` fires on every pick, `onApply` on `Apply` (and on `Today`), `onCancel` on `Cancel`. The picker does not close itself.
- Range: the first click sets the start, the second the end; a click on a day before the start, or a click after the range is complete, starts a new range. `Apply` is disabled until both ends are set.
- Keyboard: arrows move by a day or a week, `Home` / `End` to the start / end of the week, `PageUp` / `PageDown` by a month; the month changes when the focus leaves it. Only one day is in the tab order.
- Dates are local days; the week starts on Sunday (Figma). Names and the summary format come from `locale` through `Intl`.

## Figma notes

What Figma draws: March 2023 with the 16th selected (and, in `Full`, 16–25 March), `Cancel` / `Apply` or `Today`, the time picker (13:20) in `date-time`.

Not drawn, defined by analogy (AI-defined):

- Hover of a day (1px Hover Blue Light ring) and keyboard focus (`--shadow-focus`); hover and focus of the month arrows.
- `Apply` disabled while nothing is picked; empty and start-only ranges.
- Six-week months, ranges across months, a footer-less picker, locales, narrow containers.
- Dark theme: not designed — the light values are used.

Assumed / normalised:

- **Mock dates.** Figma puts the 1st of March on a Tuesday and the 16th under `We`; the real March 2023 starts on a Wednesday (the 16th is a Thursday). The component draws the real calendar, so the columns differ from the screenshot.
- **Fonts.** The day numbers are Manrope SemiBold (and one column Poppins Medium) in Figma — a font that is not in the system. All days use the base font (Poppins Medium 13px) so they stay consistent.
- **Two months.** In `Full` Figma shows the same month twice under one `Mar 2023` title. The component shows two consecutive months with the title `Mar 2023 – Apr 2023`.
- The month title is Secondary Grey in Figma (it reads as disabled); kept as designed.
- The arrows are 16px as in Figma; the hit area is widened to 32px with padding and an equal negative margin, so nothing moves.
- Range ends are rounded only on the outer side (Figma draws the 16th rounded on the left and the 25th on the right, no rounding at week breaks).
- The shadow is the existing Table Row (`0 4 4`); Figma's effect list is `0 4 2`. Same decision as for the table rows.
- The icons `chevron-left` / `chevron-right` / `xmark` / `tick` are matched by look (Figma icons are unnamed vectors).


## Accessibility

- A `role="group"` with a month `role="grid"` (weekday `columnheader`s with full names, `gridcell`s with day buttons named by the full date, e.g. "Thursday, March 16, 2023"); the picked days have `aria-selected`. The month title is `aria-live="polite"`.
- Keyboard: see Behaviour. One day is in the tab order; `Cancel` / `Apply` / `Today` are buttons.
- Contrast, computed: days `#292639` on white 14.68:1, on the range `#ECEEFB` 12.71:1; picked day white on `#1D2542` 15.05:1; `Cancel` `#1D2542` on `#ECEEFB` 13.03:1.
- ⚠️ Kept as designed: the month title and the days of the neighbouring months (`#A7A4B7` on white) are 2.43:1, below 4.5:1 for text (2.11:1 when a neighbouring day falls in a range); the range band (`#ECEEFB` on white) is 1.15:1 and the panel border 1.12:1 — below 3:1 for non-text; the hover ring (`#888CF6`) is 2.95:1; the days are 32px high (about 33px wide) and `Cancel` / `Apply` are 37px high — below the 44px touch target.
- Not covered: `min` / `max` dates and disabled days are not designed, so there are no props for them.
