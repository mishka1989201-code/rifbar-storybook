# Favicons

The app icon of Rifbar from the Figma board
[Favicons](https://www.figma.com/design/4Q7E8IQ07a9xFiNVBfmo4M/%F0%9F%93%B1-ERP-System-v-1.1--Mockups----Rifbar-2023%F0%9F%93%B1?node-id=3644-633411):
one **template** (the white sign on the purple brand gradient) and the list of sizes it is exported to.
It is a brand asset, not a UI component, so it lives in Foundations; the page redraws it from the `Logo` sign and tokens.

The Figma board has 24 exports named like this (the board repeats `ms-icon-144x144`, `android-icon-72x72` and `android-icon-192x192`).
Every export is the template scaled to a square; no size has its own drawing.

## How to use

```html
<link rel="icon" type="image/png" sizes="32x32" href="/favicon-32x32.png" />
<link rel="icon" type="image/png" sizes="16x16" href="/favicon-16x16.png" />
<link rel="apple-touch-icon" sizes="180x180" href="/apple-icon-180x180.png" />
<link rel="icon" type="image/png" sizes="192x192" href="/android-icon-192x192.png" />
<meta name="msapplication-TileImage" content="/ms-icon-144x144.png" />
```

The PNG files are **not** in the repository: the Figma export could not be downloaded (asset proxy 403). They can be rendered from the template
(`AppIcon` in `FaviconsDocs.tsx`) at every size listed above.

## Figma notes

- The gradient is the Figma colour style **#3 Button & Menu Active Gradient**: −90°, `#371A75` (right) → `#8A53BA` (left). Not used anywhere else yet.
- The corner radius is 44px on 310px (14.2%), so it is a percentage token and scales with the icon. Figma shows the small sizes with the same proportion.
- The sign in Figma is a mask over the gradient; here the same sign path as in `Logo` is used (263px tall, centred, 24px from the top). Compared with the Figma render by eye.
- Not drawn in Figma: a maskable (full-bleed) Android icon, a monochrome Safari pinned-tab icon, a dark-theme variant. Not added.
- Small sizes (16px, 32px) are the full sign scaled down; the sign becomes hard to read at 16px, kept as designed.

## Accessibility

- The icon carries no text; give it an accessible name (`aria-label`) when it is shown in the UI.
- Contrast, computed: white sign on `#8A53BA` 5.21:1, on `#371A75` 13.40:1.
- No interactive element, no touch-target issue.
